When plans change

Missing a paint from the recipe?

Use the converter to look for a close colour in another brand, then check whether it does the same job. A wash and a base paint can look similar on screen and behave very differently on a model.

Read the guide to swapping paints in a recipe

What can I try for free?

Start with up to five recipes and one project. Core recipe and project tools work offline. Paid options and local prices are shown in the app.

Will my recipes sync between devices?

Recipes and projects sync automatically between devices on the same platform, with manual backup and restore also available. Android and iOS do not have complete cross-platform sync, and manual transfers may be incomplete.

Can I share with other painters?

Community is currently available on Android. You can publish and browse posts from other miniature painters with an account and internet connection. Community is not currently available on iOS.
