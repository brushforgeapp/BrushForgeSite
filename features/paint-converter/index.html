Before colour distance

Compatibility comes first

A close recorded colour is useful only when the product behaves similarly enough for the intended job. BrushForge therefore narrows the candidate set before calculating the winning match.

1

Behaviour category

Opaque, metallic, wash, shade, Contrast, Speedpaint, Xpress, ink, glaze, and other translucent products remain distinct.

2

Grouped finish

Recorded finishes are grouped as metallic, transparent, or standard. Transparent and Translucent are treated as aliases.

3

Exact ranking

Every eligible candidate that matches both rules in the chosen brand is evaluated with CIEDE2000. The smallest distance is ranked first.

!

Separate colour reference

For a target brand with no compatible result, a different-behaviour or different-finish paint can appear only as a clearly labelled colour reference.

One-coat systems stay separateContrast, Speedpaint, and Xpress are separate behaviour categories. A metallic Speedpaint remains a Speedpaint with a metallic finish. It does not become interchangeable with an ordinary metallic acrylic or another one-coat system simply because both have a metallic finish.
Reading a result

A practical ΔE2000 guide

0

Same recorded color value

The stored colour values match. That is not evidence that the physical paints are identical.

>0–2

Very close color match

Usually difficult to distinguish under comparable viewing conditions.

>2–5

Close color match

A useful shortlist for substitution; still check behaviour and finish.

>5–10

Noticeable difference

Review the swatches and intended use carefully.

>10

Different color

Treat it as an alternative rather than an equivalent.

!

Screen preview

Finish, lighting, calibration, True Tone, Night Shift, and display technology change what you see.

Finish effects are illustrative screen cuesStandalone swatches may add a metallic reflection, gloss or satin highlight, transparent checker, or subtle matte texture. These visual cues do not change the recorded colour, CIEDE2000 calculation, compatibility check, or ranking. Direct comparison strips stay flat.
Use colour, behaviour, and grouped finish togetherMetallics, washes, transparent one-coat paints, and standard opaque acrylics are not interchangeable just because their recorded colour is close. Coverage, opacity, hue bias, finish, undercoat, and use case can matter as much as hue.
