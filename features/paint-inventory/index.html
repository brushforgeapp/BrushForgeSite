Before you start

A few useful things to know.

What can I try for free?

Start with up to 40 owned paints. The core collection tools work offline, so you can check your list in a shop with poor reception. Paid options and local prices are shown in the app.

Can I bring an existing collection?

Import options differ between Android, iOS and app versions. Check the formats your version accepts, keep your original list, and review the imported paints before relying on the new collection.

Read the collection and import guide

Will my collection sync between devices?

Collections sync automatically between devices on the same platform. Manual backup and restore are also available. Android and iOS do not have complete cross-platform sync, and a manual transfer may be incomplete. Keep a backup before changing platforms.
