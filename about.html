About the App

What is BrushForge?

A paint converter and painting toolkit that keeps the practical parts of a project together.

Independent app by Bas Mahieu

Built by a miniature painter.

I’m Bas, a UX/UI designer, developer, and miniature painter. BrushForge started as a small tool for my own late-night painting sessions and grew into an independent iOS and Android side project.

I still maintain the app and paint catalogue with help from painter feedback. Expanding paint coverage is the main focus right now, including direct collaboration with paint brands.

Read why I built BrushForge

The Mission

Miniature painting should be about creativity, not wrestling with paint conversions across brands, trying to remember what paints you own while at the hobby shop, or losing track of the recipes you used for an army you started six months ago.

BrushForge was built to be one practical tool that handles the logistics so you can focus entirely on the brush.

Core Features

Catalogue Matching

BrushForge uses ΔE2000 in LAB color space to compare catalogue screen colors across its 4,300+ paint app database. That catalogue is only the start: more ranges and product details are being added as the app grows. Physical finish, lighting, and application can still change how a paint appears.

Complete Organization

Carry your entire inventory in your pocket. Track what you own, manage a shopping wishlist, log your custom mixes, and build step-by-step project plans for your miniatures.

Creative Tools

Feeling stuck? Use our Recipe Generator to find ideas based on your existing paints, or analyze photos to pull matching color schemes directly from reference images.

Offline First

Painting often happens in basements, at events, or in the back of hobby shops with terrible reception. Core BrushForge tools work offline, so they remain available without a connection.

Platform & Pricing

BrushForge is proudly available natively on both iOS and Android.

The app is free to start with access to the converter and basic organization tools. The starting limits are 40 paints, five recipes, and one project. On Android, Premium removes ads and increases paint collection and project allowances. Exact paid allowances and local prices are shown in the current app and store listing.

Data syncs automatically between devices on the same platform, with manual backup and restore as an additional option. Android and iOS do not have complete cross-platform sync, and manual transfers between them may be incomplete. Purchases remain tied to their platform.

A long-term roadmap goal is to add web accounts and app sync so saved work can eventually be used from a browser. No release date has been announced.

Paid options directly support the solo development of BrushForge and its ongoing maintenance.
