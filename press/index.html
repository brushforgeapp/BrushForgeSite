One-line descriptionBrushForge is an offline-friendly iOS and Android toolkit for miniature painters to compare recorded paint colours across brands, track owned paints, save recipes, and plan projects.

Product summary

BrushForge brings paint conversion and painting organization into one native app. Its app catalogue covers 4,300+ paints and growing. Expanding paint coverage is the current main focus, including direct collaboration with paint brands. The core product connects catalogue-based CIEDE2000 matching with owned-paint inventory, repeatable recipes, and project planning. It is free to start and available on Android and iOS.

The public website provides an account-free converter, 2,941 generated paint pages, and brand-to-brand conversion charts from the current 10 July 2026 web snapshot. Public matches are catalogue-based and are not presented as physical brushout measurements. Web accounts and app sync are a long-term roadmap goal without an announced release date.

Paint conversion, collection, recipes, projects, palettes, and photo colour tools are available on Android and iOS. Community is currently Android only. Reviewers should still identify the build and platform they tested because import paths and release timing can differ.

Community

Community lets Android users publish and browse posts from other miniature painters. It requires an account and internet connection. Community is not currently available in the iOS app or as a public website account.

Known limitations

  • Colour matching compares stored catalogue values; it is not a database of controlled physical brushouts.
  • ΔE2000 describes recorded colour distance, not coverage, opacity, flow, finish, or metallic reflectance.
  • Screen swatches vary with calibration, display technology, colour-shifting settings, and lighting.
  • The public website catalogue and native app catalogue are separate surfaces and can update on different schedules.
  • Data syncs automatically within the same platform, but Android and iOS do not have complete cross-platform sync. Manual transfers may be incomplete.
  • Community is currently Android only, and import paths can differ by platform and version.

Founder bio

Bas Mahieu is a UX/UI designer with a programming background and a miniature painter. BrushForge began as a personal tool after he returned to miniature painting following a long break. It remains an independently developed side project, shaped by painter feedback and the slow work of collecting, cleaning, and maintaining paint data.
