RLM 63: similar hobby colours

AK Interactive · 3rd Gen Acrylic Air · AK11816

Catalogue data updated 11 September 2026

Looking for a hobby paint close to AK RLM 63? Two Thin Coats Rodent Grey is the closer of these two catalogue colours. Army Painter Army Green is another option if you already have the original Warpaints bottle.

AK's AIR range covers aircraft colours and can be used with a brush or an airbrush. See AK's 3GEN paint guide (PDF) for the range guidance. The colour comparisons below come from BrushForge's catalogue.

Similar hobby colours to try

Before you use it on a model

Paint a small patch beside the colour you want to replace, using the same primer on both. Let it dry before comparing it with the other colours in your scheme. The Army Green listed here is WP1110 from the original Warpaints range; don't assume a newer bottle with a similar name will behave the same way.
