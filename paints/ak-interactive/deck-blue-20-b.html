Looking for a hobby-paint alternative to AK Deck Blue 20 B (AK5001)? Vallejo German Grey and Pro Acryl Coal Black are nearby recorded colours. The original is recorded as satin, while both options are matte, so the sheen will need attention too.

Similar hobby colours to try

Before you use it on a model

Paint a test on the same primer and try your planned varnish before judging the result. For a historical ship, also check references for the vessel and period: a small colour-distance score does not prove that a paint matches the original naval specification.
