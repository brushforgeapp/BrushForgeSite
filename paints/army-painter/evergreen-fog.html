How to read these matches

CIEDE2000 compares recorded catalogue colors: a smaller ΔE means closer recorded color values. Aim for ΔE ≤ 5 as a shortlist, not a guarantee of a physical paint match.

On-screen swatches are useful for relative comparison, then a dried physical swatch is the real check. Screen appearance varies with finish, lighting, True Tone or Night Shift, calibration and display technology.

Coverage, opacity, hue bias, finish, undercoat, product type and intended use can change the painted result. BrushForge keeps opaque colors, metallics, washes, shades, inks, glazes, Contrast, Speedpaint and Xpress Color in compatible type and finish groups. Finish effects on swatches are visual cues only and never affect the color calculation. Test under the lighting and surface that matter to the project. Read the matching methodology.
