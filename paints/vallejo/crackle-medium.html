Crackle Medium

Vallejo · Arte Deco · 84.160

Working on a weathered surface? Crackle Medium is an effect medium for creating cracks in a painted finish. Its job is the texture, so choosing it by a colour swatch would miss the point.

Medium Crackled effects

Vallejo's Arte Deco chart lists number 160 as Medium Craquelar, with the English label Weathering Medium. Check the bottle code when following an older tutorial: Crackle Base 161 and Crackle Glaze 611 are separate entries.

Before you use it

  1. Try the intended paint layers on a spare piece first. Check whether the size of the cracks suits your model or scenery.
  2. Follow the layering and drying instructions for your bottle. A tutorial for another crackle product may use a different method.
  3. Keep the test piece and a note of the layers. It is much easier to repeat an effect when you know how you made it.

Why there is no colour match

A close colour value cannot tell you whether another medium will make the same cracks. Compare the intended effect and application method instead.
