Midnight Blue: similar hobby colours

Catalogue data updated 11 September 2026

The name is a little misleading: Vallejo's Arte Deco chart shows Midnight Blue 85.070 as a very dark green-blue. That helps explain why Citadel Lupercal Green and AK Archaic Turquoise appear here, rather than a straightforward navy blue.

Colour names checked against Vallejo's Arte Deco chart (PDF). The comparisons below use BrushForge catalogue data.

Before you use it on a model

Put a dry test patch under your painting light before committing. With colours this dark, compare a second patch mixed with a little of the same pale paint; it makes the blue-green balance easier to see. A close screen colour still needs a brush test.
