Satin Varnish

Vallejo · Arte Deco · 84.169

Satin Varnish is the option to try when you want some sheen without a full gloss finish. A small sample is a better guide than a swatch on a screen.

Varnish Satin finish

This page covers Arte Deco 84.169. Check that code on your bottle; Vallejo sells satin varnishes in several ranges.

Before you use it

  1. Test it over your actual paint layers and wait until the sample is dry before comparing the sheen.
  2. If you are deciding between matt, satin and gloss, use separate patches under the same light.
  3. Follow the application and drying instructions on the bottle. Save your choice with the recipe so later models get the same finish.

Why there is no colour match

The useful comparison here is the dried sheen and how you apply the product. A colour-distance score would not answer either question.
