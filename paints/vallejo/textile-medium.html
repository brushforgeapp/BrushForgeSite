Textile Medium

Vallejo · Arte Deco · 84.159

Textile Medium is an auxiliary for painting fabric. Vallejo lists 84.159 for use with textile colour and other acrylic colours, rather than as a standalone paint colour.

Medium Fabric painting

For a fabric banner or another cloth project, start with a scrap of the same material. A sculpted cloak is a different surface: looking like cloth does not make a model textile.

Before you use it

  1. Check the instructions for your bottle and the paint you are mixing it with before choosing a ratio.
  2. Try the mix on a matching fabric scrap. Once it is dry, check the feel and flex as well as the colour.
  3. For a washable project, follow the product instructions for fixing and washing. Do not borrow those steps from a different medium.

Why there is no colour match

A colour match says nothing about how a medium works on fabric. Check its intended use and test it with your chosen material and paint.
