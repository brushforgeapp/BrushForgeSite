How to read these matches

CIEDE2000 compares recorded catalogue colors: a smaller ΔE means closer recorded color values. Aim for ΔE ≤5 as a shortlist, not a guarantee of a physical paint match.

On-screen swatches are useful for relative comparison, then a dried physical swatch is the real check. Screen appearance varies with finish, lighting, True Tone or Night Shift, calibration, and display technology.

Coverage, opacity, hue bias, finish, undercoat, product type, and intended use can change the painted result. Opaque colors, metallics, washes, inks, glazes, and Contrast, Speedpaint, or Xpress-style single-coat products are not behaviorally interchangeable. Compare the same behavior family and test under the lighting and surface that matter to the project. Read the matching methodology.

Common questions

Why does the website list fewer paints than the app?
The public web catalogue contains 2,941 selected records across opaque, metallic, wash, ink, glaze, single-coat, and other translucent families. The app catalogue contains 4,300+ paints and growing, including additional product types and ranges. Expanding the catalogue is the main focus right now, with more ranges being added through research and direct work with paint brands.
How should I use the ΔE2000 results?
Use them as a catalogue-based starting point for comparing screen colors. Paint finish, lighting, application, and display calibration still matter, so confirm important choices with the physical paint.
What works offline?
The core library, collection, recipe, and palette tools work offline. Connected features and purchases require an internet connection.
Can I import my existing paint collection?
The current iOS listing documents inventory CSV and selected paint-tracker imports, BrushForge JSON backup restore, and photo-assisted paint import with a review step. Availability depends on platform and app release.
What is included when I start free?
Android Free includes up to 40 paints, five recipes, one project, the converter, and core organization tools. Android Premium removes ads and increases paint collection and project allowances. Exact paid allowances and local prices are shown in the app and Google Play. iOS paid options can differ.
Does data sync between Android and iOS?
Data syncs automatically between devices on the same platform, and manual backup and restore are also available. Android and iOS do not have complete cross-platform sync. A manual transfer may move some data, but it can be incomplete, and purchases remain tied to their platform.
