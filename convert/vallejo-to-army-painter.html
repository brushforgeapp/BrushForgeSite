How to read these matches

CIEDE2000 compares recorded catalogue colors: a smaller ΔE means closer recorded color values. Aim for ΔE ≤ 5 as a shortlist, not a guarantee of a physical paint match.

On-screen swatches are useful for relative comparison, then a dried physical swatch is the real check. Screen appearance varies with finish, lighting, True Tone or Night Shift, calibration and display technology.

Coverage, opacity, hue bias, finish, undercoat, product type and intended use can change the painted result. Opaque colors, metallics, washes, inks, glazes, and Contrast, Speedpaint or Xpress-style single-coat products are not behaviorally interchangeable. Compare the same behavior family and test under the lighting and surface that matter to the project. Read the matching methodology.

Frequently asked questions

Can I use Army Painter paints instead of Vallejo?

How accurate is this chart?
Matches are computed from catalogue screen colors in LAB space with CIEDE2000. Opaque colors, metallics, washes, inks, glazes and Contrast, Speedpaint or Xpress-style single-coat products are kept in separate behavior families. Finish, opacity, hue bias, coverage, undercoat, lighting and physical paint behavior are not captured by a flat screen color.
What does the ΔE number mean?
A smaller CIEDE2000 value means closer recorded colors. The tiers are: 0, Same recorded color value; >0–2, Very close color match; >2–5, Close color match; >5–10, Noticeable difference; and >10, Different color. Aim for 5 or less as a shortlist, not a guarantee of matching physical paint behavior.
Why does a row show a warned reference instead of an equivalent?
BrushForge keeps product behaviors separate: opaque color, metallic, wash, ink, glaze and Contrast, Speedpaint or Xpress-style single-coat paint. 8 rows have no same-behavior Army Painter product in this web catalogue. Where another product in the broad color pool exists, its nearest recorded color may be shown with a behavior warning; it is not called compatible. Airbrush lines, sprays, primers and technical products are outside this chart.
