How to read these matches

CIEDE2000 compares recorded catalogue colors: a smaller ΔE means closer recorded color values. Aim for ΔE ≤ 5 as a shortlist, not a guarantee of a physical paint match.

On-screen swatches are useful for relative comparison, then a dried physical swatch is the real check. Screen appearance varies with finish, lighting, True Tone or Night Shift, calibration and display technology.

Coverage, opacity, hue bias, finish, undercoat, product type and intended use can change the painted result. BrushForge keeps opaque colors, metallics, washes, shades, inks, glazes, Contrast, Speedpaint and Xpress Color in compatible type and finish groups. Finish effects on swatches are visual cues only and never affect the color calculation. Test under the lighting and surface that matter to the project. Read the matching methodology.

Frequently asked questions

Can I use Citadel paints instead of Pro Acryl?
This chart assesses 119 Pro Acryl paints. 119 have a compatible Citadel product in the included web catalogue, and 45 of those catalogue-color comparisons have a CIEDE2000 distance of 5 or less. Check a dried physical swatch before substituting a paint.
How accurate is this chart?
Matches are computed from catalogue screen colors in LAB space with CIEDE2000. Opaque colors, metallics, washes, inks, glazes and Contrast, Speedpaint or Xpress-style single-coat products are kept in separate paint-behavior and grouped-finish families. Finish, opacity, hue bias, coverage, undercoat, lighting and physical paint behavior are not captured by a flat screen color.
What does the ΔE number mean?
A smaller CIEDE2000 value means closer recorded colors. The tiers are: 0, Same recorded color value; >0–2, Very close color match; >2–5, Close color match; >5–10, Noticeable difference; and >10, Different color. Aim for 5 or less as a shortlist, not a guarantee of matching physical paint behavior.
Why does a row show a warned reference instead of an equivalent?
BrushForge keeps opaque color, metallic, wash, shade, ink, glaze, Contrast, Speedpaint and Xpress Color types separate, then also applies grouped finish compatibility. 0 rows have no compatible Citadel product in this web catalogue. Where another product in the broad color pool exists, its nearest recorded color may be shown with a compatibility warning; it is not called compatible. Airbrush lines, sprays, primers and technical products are outside this chart.
