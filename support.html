Purchases and subscriptions

Android purchases are processed by Google Play; iOS purchases are processed by Apple. Use Restore Purchases in the app where available. Manage or cancel subscriptions in the same store account used to purchase. Refund decisions are handled by the relevant store.

  • Manage Google Play subscriptions
  • Apple billing and subscriptions

Imports, backups, and sync

Current iOS releases support inventory CSV and supported tracker imports, plus supported BrushForge JSON backup restores with duplicate prevention. Available import paths can differ by platform and version. Platform-specific sync does not currently move data between Android and iOS, so keep a current export before changing device or platform.

Report a paint or match issue

Include the source paint, suggested paint, both product lines and codes where available, the displayed ΔE2000 value, and what appears inaccurate. Catalogue matches use recorded color values; physical finish and behavior can differ.
