How sync works today

Paint conversion, inventory, recipes, projects, palettes, and photo colour tools are available on Android and iOS. Community is currently available on Android only. Data syncs automatically between devices on the same platform, and manual backup and restore are also available. Android and iOS do not have complete cross-platform sync. Manual transfers between them may be incomplete, subscriptions can differ, and purchases remain tied to their platform.

What you can do

Find an alternative paint
Use catalogue-based ΔE2000 comparison as a shortlist, with paint type and screen-preview caveats shown alongside results.
Check your collection
Track owned and wishlisted paints so the app can support decisions at your desk or in the hobby store.
Keep recipes and projects together
Record steps, palettes, notes, and project plans instead of rebuilding the workflow from memory.
Work offline
The paint library, collection, recipes, palettes, and core tools are offline-friendly. Account services, purchases, Community, and connected photo suggestions require internet access.

Community is currently on Android

Android users can publish and browse Community posts from other miniature painters. Community uses an account and internet connection. It is not currently available in the iOS app.
