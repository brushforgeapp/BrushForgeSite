A few things to know

Paint conversion, inventory, recipes, projects, palettes and photo colour tools are available on both platforms. Core painting tools work offline; account services, purchases and Community need an internet connection.

Same-platform sync and manual backup options are available. Check your plan in the app for included features. Android and iOS do not have complete cross-platform sync. Transfers between them may be incomplete, and purchases stay with the platform where you bought them.

Paint comparisons use recorded catalogue colours. They help you choose what to try, but finish, coverage and the colour on your actual model can differ.

Community is currently on Android

Android users can publish and browse Community posts from other miniature painters. Community uses an account and internet connection. It is not currently available in the iOS app.
