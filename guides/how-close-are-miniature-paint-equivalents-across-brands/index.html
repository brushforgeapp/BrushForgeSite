How close are miniature paint equivalents across brands?

An aggregate look at the nearest compatible catalogue colors in the approved BrushForge web catalogue.

Written by Bas Mahieu · Published 3 September 2026 · Updated 3 September 2026

Short answer: 6,242 of 14,879 nearest compatible cross-brand comparisons (42.0%) fall at ΔE2000 5 or less. That makes them useful shortlist candidates, not guaranteed physical-paint replacements. Finish, opacity, coverage, undercoat and product behavior can still change the result.

What was compared

For each match-eligible source paint, BrushForge selected at most one nearest compatible result in every other included brand. Opaque paints, metallics, washes, shades and other translucent systems stay in separate behavior and grouped-finish pools. The figures below use the approved public catalogue dated 10 July 2026.

A low color distance is only one part of substitution. Metallic flake, reflectivity and sheen are not represented by a flat swatch. Washes and shades also depend on flow, staining and pooling. Single-coat systems can behave differently even when their recorded colors are close.

How to use an equivalent well

  1. Start with results in the same paint behavior and grouped finish.
  2. Use ΔE2000 5 or less as a shortlist, not as proof of identical paint.
  3. Check coverage, opacity, finish, hue bias and intended use.
  4. Test a dried physical swatch on the undercoat and surface that matter.

Sources and limitations

The distance calculation follows the CIEDE2000 formula described by the CIE and is tested against Gaurav Sharma's published reference data. The underlying inputs are recorded catalogue screen colors, not a claim of laboratory measurement of dried paint.
