Catalogue notes behind the advice

The public BrushForge catalogue snapshot dated 10 July 2026 contains 2,941 paint records across eight brands. Its behaviour families contain 2,154 opaque, 274 metallic, 301 single-coat, 148 wash, 42 ink, 10 glaze, and 12 other translucent records. A compatible match must stay inside the source family; a different translucent family can appear only as a clearly warned colour reference.

The native app catalogue has 4,300+ paints and is still growing. Adding more brands and ranges is the current main focus, including direct work with paint brands. The public web count and app count refer to different product surfaces; they should not be compared as if every record and workflow is identical.
